The product

Business-development research, not clinical intelligence.

The product is designed to help vendors decide where a public change justifies deeper account research.

Facility-change watch

Track dated public changes around target SNF accounts.

Source-linked brief

Show what changed, when, and the public evidence behind it.

Commercial relevance

Explain why the change may matter to the vendor's offer while keeping inference separate from fact.

Best fit

Best for small and regional vendors selling into skilled nursing and post-acute accounts.

  • You already have a defined territory or target account list.
  • A meaningful facility change can alter sales timing or account priority.
  • You want public-source research without PHI, EHR, or employer data.

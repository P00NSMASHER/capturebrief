Common questions

Here is what to expect.

What do I get for $79 a month?

A watch for relevant NYC restaurant openings and project changes, based on public records. Results are sorted for your type of business and area, with a report and a CSV file you can use in a spreadsheet.

Does every listed restaurant need my service?

No. A record gives you a reason to check a project. It does not prove the restaurant is opening soon, shopping for your service or ready to buy.

Will I get the same old projects again?

The regular watch focuses on new or important changes after your start date. A recent starter list, when requested, is labeled separately. Missing or unavailable records are not treated as proof that nothing changed.

Is PermitPlate a license-plate scanner?

No. It helps businesses find restaurant projects in New York City. It does not scan vehicles or check deliveries.

Good questions

Know what to expect.

Is a flagged amount money recovered?

No. Identified variance, a reviewed finding, an issued credit and a realized recovery are different states. The report keeps them separate.

How much does a pilot cost?

Scope and fees are agreed in writing before work begins. The website does not quote a universal fee or promise a savings amount.

Can I upload or email my invoices here?

No. Do not send confidential freight documents through this page or ordinary email. We agree a controlled data route before accepting customer files.

What happens to an earlier free 20-invoice offer?

Previously offered free samples keep their original terms. A new pilot does not retroactively change an existing offer.

Will you contact carriers or move money?

Not as part of an initial review without separate, specific approval. Your team decides what to pursue after reviewing the evidence.

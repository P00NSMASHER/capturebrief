The product

A defensible reviewed discrepancy report.

The goal is not to flood finance or transportation teams with anomaly flags. It is to show which discrepancies are supportable, which need more evidence, and exactly how the expected charge was reproduced.

01

Controlling authority

Tie each asserted finding to the applicable rate, agreement, amendment, or other supported pricing authority.

02

Reproducible calculation

Show the shipment/invoice inputs, expected charge, observed charge, and exact variance rather than a black-box score.

03

Honest disposition

Separate clean, supported, unresolved, excluded, and review states so low evidence coverage cannot masquerade as a clean bill of health.

Blind audit method

Prove the incremental result, not just the existence of flags.

The first product is a bounded acceptance test. A clean result is still a valid report; it is not evidence that money was recovered.

1
Freeze scope and authorityDefine the population, period, carrier/mode, currency, governing rates/amendments, and required shipment evidence.
2
Seal the incumbent resultWhere agreed, freeze the existing audit/payment result before challenger review so comparison remains fair.
3
Reproduce and reviewCalculate expected charges from supported authority, then independently review asserted findings and a predeclared sample of non-flags.
4
Deliver the decision packetReturn the complete disposition register, supported finding packets, unresolved-evidence requests, and the blind comparison.

What the buyer can inspect

Every asserted finding should survive a skeptical review.

A supported finding packet identifies the invoice or shipment, controlling term and effective version, source page/cell/segment, reproducible expected charge, observed charge, exact discrepancy, and next decision.

Freight invoice review

Find freight billing discrepancies you can actually explain.

FreightRecovery compares an agreed invoice population with the governing rates and shipment evidence, then returns a reviewed discrepancy report. Missing or ambiguous evidence stays visible instead of being turned into a savings claim.

The product

The report is the product.

The goal is not to flood you with flags. It is to show which discrepancies are supportable, which need more evidence, and what the calculation is.

Authority first

Tie each asserted finding to the applicable rate, agreement, amendment, or supported rule.

Blind comparison

Where agreed, freeze the incumbent result before opening it so the challenger can be compared fairly.

Outcome separation

Keep identified variance, validated finding, issued credit, and realized recovery as separate states.

Best fit

Best for manufacturers, distributors, 3PLs, and freight-payment teams with enough complexity to justify an independent check.

  • You can identify who owns freight cost and invoice review.
  • The relevant rates and shipment records are accessible.
  • You want an explainable benchmark, not a black-box anomaly score.

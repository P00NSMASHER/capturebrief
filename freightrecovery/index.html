Common questions

Here is what to expect.

Will you get money back for us?

A review can identify charges worth questioning, but it cannot promise a refund. We report a possible error, a confirmed finding, a credit and money received separately.

What does the first review cost?

We agree on the work and fee in writing before it starts. The price depends on the bills, agreements and records involved.

Can I email you my freight bills?

Not yet. Start with a general description of the problem. Do not attach confidential bills or contracts. We agree how customer files will be handled before accepting them.

What about an earlier free 20-invoice offer?

Previously offered free samples keep their original terms. This page does not change an offer you already received.

Will you contact the shipping company?

Not without separate, specific approval. You control what happens after the review. We do not move money as part of the initial review.

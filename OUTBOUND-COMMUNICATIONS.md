# Outbound communication approval

Effective September 21, 2026. Owner instruction; supersedes prior broad outreach permission.

Every external email or message requires the owner's explicit approval of that specific
message before it is sent. This includes replies, follow-ups, new outreach, reviewer
correspondence, customer deliveries, and source-change notifications.

Present the recipient, channel, subject where applicable, complete proposed body, and
attachments before requesting approval. Approval applies only to that recipient and
message content. Changed recipients, wording, or attachments require fresh approval.

An inbound reply, a completed review, a passing release gate, a signed-up prospect, or
"continue working" is not permission to send. A prior approval is not an allowance for
future messages. No silent follow-ups or automated watch notifications are authorized.

Internal drafting, testing, repository maintenance, report generation, and read-only
status checks can continue within the active task. Do not make purchases, accept paid
consulting engagements, or other external commitments without specific authorization.

The existing cross-business one-outreach-until-reply rule remains an additional ceiling,
not a substitute for message-specific approval.

The fulfillment planner always reports `external_send_authorized = false`. It has no
sender and performs no external action. This policy is not a claim that unrelated apps
or pre-existing scheduled tasks have been technically reconfigured.

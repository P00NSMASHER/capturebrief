# CaptureBrief first-customer launch readiness: internal preparation, no customer required

**Scope:** a supervised, no-card, single-assumption free-check pilot for up to ten
businesses. This assessment is intentionally **before any customer**. No sales,
customer feedback, customer approval, completed report for a real customer,
customer receipt, or paid conversion is a prelaunch requirement.

## Two different go/no-go decisions

**A. Pre-first-customer OPERATING PREPARATION** (can be rehearsed without anyone):
1. No-submit public inquiry page is demonstrably live and independently tested
   against the correct Cloudflare deployment revision.
2. No-card, one-assumption and ten-business reservation controls pass offline QA,
   including alias, concurrency, charge and unintended-delivery negatives.
3. Official first-party current-source retrieval and a non-sensitive public
   review sample work. A valid sample may end in **HOLD** when the original
   presents conflicting dates or cannot show all amendments. A genuine conflict
   found and properly reported is a useful result, not a test failure.
4. Source-action/history, deletion-aware resource and incorporated-rule
   verification software is tested on adversarial fictional fixtures. Ability
   to demand per-case missing evidence is required; proving all history for
   an as-yet-unknown buyer solicitation is not.
5. Use the approved **PermitPlate-style private GitHub cloud** with an isolated `P00NSMASHER/capturebrief-state` repository and CaptureBrief-only token, plus an encrypted primary operator workspace. Provider selected does not mean its private repo, key custody or independent restore is already provisioned. See CAPTUREBRIEF-PRIVATE-GITHUB-RECOVERY.md. Provide restricted access
   and a retention/deletion policy. Complete an actual **independently downloaded
   off-device encrypted restore using non-customer rehearsal data**, and retain
   evidence with a recovery key held separately from the primary device.
   CI test-key restores cannot substitute for this operator-owned rehearsal.
6. Rehearse private intake, operator-owned report preparation, protected
   one-message release authorization and reliable rollback/denial, with zero
   messages sent. A customer must not be created or impersonated to pass QA.

**B. PER-CASE DELIVERY**, only when an actual customer request arrives:
- Confirm genuine inbound request, verified legal business identity, one
  specific SAM.gov pursuit, exact one-assumption scope and consent.
- Reserve one of ten business slots with the existing irreversible registry.
- Independently establish first-party current notice, full family action and
  amendment history, deletion-inclusive attachment inventory, incorporated
  rule editions and material evidence. Missing originals or unresolved
  controlling interpretation remain HOLD, not a marketing claim.
- Complete the actual full report; internal human reviewer verifies exact
  report bytes/citations. Customer review or customer approval of the report
  is **not** an upstream prerequisite, though customer-requested corrections
  can be handled later. The owner separately approves the exact recipient,
  body, attachment and *each* external delivery.
- Retain approved private source originals, registry, release ledger and
  off-device encrypted backup. Independently validate actual customer
  recovery after necessary materials exist. Source/report content remains
  private. Never auto-send or auto-charge.

## Honest current limits

The October 28 authenticated Treasury RFQ PDF and cross-runner test restore
prove a working first-party source path and a **fictional/public-data** restore
candidate. They do **not** prove full official action history, independently
held production recovery-key custody, owner-approved customer storage or an
actual off-device operator-run restore. The Government's public Opportunities
v2 API is latest-active only; archived Data Services and human-reviewed
first-party version evidence are needed separately for the exact pursuit.

**Product positioning:** readiness to **prepare** for the first customer
is not proof of automated delivery or paid launch. Full-catalog coverage
is not mandatory for the bounded founder-curated inquiry lane, but a case
whose complete history cannot be independently established stays HOLD or
must be declined. Preserve fail-closed release and no-outreach controls.

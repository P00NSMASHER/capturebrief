Service terms and boundaries

Last updated October 22, 2026

Buy directly or check scope first. A direct payment reserves one review but does not guarantee that the request fits. If JP Enterprises declines a paid request before work begins, the $149 payment is refunded in full.

Operator and offer

CaptureBrief is a founder-operated service from JP Enterprises. The current public offer is a $149 one-time review of one selected public federal technology pursuit and up to five non-sensitive buyer assumptions, with a human-reviewed brief, an inspectable public-source evidence trail, and a 14-day targeted public-source change watch. There is no automatic renewal.

Direct purchase and free scope check

A buyer may use secure Stripe checkout immediately or request a free scope check first. Direct payment reserves one review but does not mean the assignment is accepted. Complete intake includes a public opportunity link, current GO/HOLD/PASS/UNSURE posture, up to five non-sensitive assumptions, and a reachable contact email. Within two business days of complete intake, JP Enterprises provides a written accept-or-decline decision, agreed scope, sources, exclusions, and delivery date. If a request is declined, or the buyer does not accept the proposed written scope, the $149 payment is refunded in full before work begins. Buyers may cancel for a full refund before substantive work starts. Business days exclude weekends and U.S. federal holidays.

Delivery, cancellation, refunds, and corrections

An accepted brief is delivered by the date agreed in writing, no later than five business days after the written scope is accepted unless the buyer affirmatively agrees in writing to a later date before work begins. If that deadline is missed, the buyer may request a full refund instead of continued performance. If official sources are unavailable or a material limitation emerges, JP Enterprises discloses the limitation and offers an agreed revised date or a full refund. A buyer who paid but never submitted complete intake may request a full refund before work begins. Report factual errors by replying to the delivered brief; citation and calculation errors within the agreed scope are corrected without an additional charge. No hidden or later-added terms shorten these refund rights. Rights under applicable law remain unaffected.

Public-source evidence review

The customer supplies a current GO, HOLD, PASS, or UNSURE posture and up to five non-sensitive assumptions. CaptureBrief tests those assumptions against the agreed public sources. Official notices, amendments, solicitation documents, and agency communications remain authoritative. The customer is responsible for checking controlling requirements before acting or submitting.

No legal advice, certification, or award guarantee

CaptureBrief does not provide legal advice, make binding eligibility determinations, certify proposal compliance, submit bids, estimate win probability, or guarantee an award, revenue, savings, or return on investment. Findings support the customer’s review; final pursuit and procurement decisions remain with the customer.

Customer responsibilities

The customer is responsible for providing an accurate public link, stating the assumptions to be checked, reviewing the delivered evidence, monitoring official sources, and obtaining qualified legal, procurement, security, or other professional advice when needed. The customer must not provide information it is not authorized to share.

Sensitive and restricted information

Do not send CUI, classified or export-controlled information, credentials, proprietary client material, nonpublic proposal content, pricing or strategy, protected source-selection information, or material from eBuy, SEWP QRT, another contract-holder portal, a private acquisition system, or another restricted source. A request that depends on such material may be declined or marked source-limited.

14-day targeted watch

The watch covers agreed public sources for fourteen calendar days after delivery of the accepted baseline. It includes baseline verification plus at least two separately recorded checks, normally around calendar days 7 and 14 (or the next business day when a checkpoint falls on a weekend or federal holiday). The customer receives a concise closeout notice, including a no-change result when appropriate. A confirmed material change affecting an agreed assumption is reported within one business day after human verification; source outages are disclosed rather than represented as successful checks. Monitoring is scheduled and targeted, not continuous or exhaustive. Earlier findings and the customer's bid posture are not silently changed. Customers remain responsible for monitoring official sources.

Ownership and permitted use

The customer retains rights in materials it lawfully provides. JP Enterprises retains its site content, methods, templates, software, and pre-existing materials. Unless the written scope states otherwise, the delivered review may be used internally by the purchasing customer for its own capture and pursuit decision-making.

Fictional example

The interactive Decision Evidence example and its source files are invented demonstrations. They are not customer work, government records, testimonials, legal or procurement advice, or proof of commercial outcomes.

No government affiliation

CaptureBrief is a private service. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by SAM.gov, the General Services Administration, the Department of Defense, or any other U.S. government entity.

Questions

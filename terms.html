Service terms and boundaries

Last updated September 15, 2026

Before payment: every founding pilot receives written scope confirmation covering the responsible contracting operator, source coverage, delivery timing, review process, cancellation/refund treatment, deliverable expectations, and any additional commercial terms that apply.

Independent service

CaptureBrief is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by SAM.gov, the General Services Administration, the Department of Defense, or any other U.S. government entity.

Public-source Pursuit QA

The current founding pilot is limited to an independent public-source QA pass on one federal opportunity already under consideration. Official notices, amendments, solicitation documents, and agency communications remain authoritative. Customers are responsible for verifying controlling requirements before acting or submitting.

No legal, procurement, eligibility, or award guarantee

CaptureBrief does not provide legal advice, make binding eligibility determinations, submit bids, promise opportunity matches, estimate win probability, or guarantee contract awards or revenue. Final procurement decisions remain with the customer.

Founding pilot

The current public offer is a supervised $149 one-time founding pilot for one selected public federal IT/cyber/cloud/DevSecOps/software/data/modernization pursuit, an independent source-linked Pursuit QA handoff with an inspectable audit appendix, and 14 days of scheduled checks of agreed public sources. There is no automatic renewal. The pilot is not self-serve: scope, source coverage, review process, delivery timing, and written commercial terms are confirmed before a buyer is invited to payment.

Scope, cancellation, refunds, and delivery acceptance

CaptureBrief does not request payment for work it has declined as out of scope. For a pilot that is accepted, the written scope confirmation sent before payment will state the expected delivery window, what constitutes the agreed deliverable, how cancellation/refund questions will be handled, and how factual corrections or delivery issues should be raised. Those written pilot-specific terms control if they differ from this general page. If you do not accept them, do not proceed to payment.

Confidentiality and sensitive information

The current founding pilot is intentionally not designed to receive confidential proposal content, CUI, classified information, export-controlled material, credentials, or proprietary client information. Do not send those materials. Any future engagement that requires confidential-information handling would require separate written terms and an appropriate workflow.

Ownership and internal use

You retain rights in materials you lawfully provide. CaptureBrief retains its site content, methodology, templates, and pre-existing materials. Unless the written scope confirmation states otherwise, a delivered qualification artifact may be used internally by the purchasing customer for its own capture and pursuit decision-making.

Monitoring limitation

Scheduled public-source checks are not continuous, exhaustive, or a replacement for the bidder’s responsibility to monitor official sources for amendments, notices, and deadlines.

Additional commercial terms

Any pilot-specific limitation of liability, governing-law provision, billing detail, delivery acceptance term, or other commercial condition not stated on this page will be presented in writing before payment. CaptureBrief does not rely on hidden post-payment terms to define the founding pilot.

Data handling

Keep the input public and the qualification inspectable.

Current founding-pilot boundary · September 15, 2026

Simple rule: if the information should not be publicly available, do not send it to the current CaptureBrief founding-pilot workflow.

Appropriate inputs

  • Public company website and capability information
  • Public SAM.gov notices
  • Public solicitation attachments and amendments
  • Public contract-vehicle, NAICS, PSC, or market information
  • Publicly releasable questions about fit, blockers, deadlines, and requirements

Do not send

  • Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)
  • Classified information
  • Export-controlled information
  • Credentials, secrets, or access tokens
  • Proprietary customer/client information
  • Nonpublic proposal drafts, sensitive pricing, or protected source-selection information

How sources are treated

CaptureBrief distinguishes controlling/current sources from supporting signals. Supporting pages may help identify capability overlap or questions to verify, but they do not replace the official solicitation or direct confirmation of eligibility-critical facts.

Access and service providers

CaptureBrief is currently founder-operated. Access to inquiry and pilot information is limited to the operator and service providers needed to host the site, communicate, and process an invited payment. The planned Netlify deployment may process ordinary site-access/security logs; Google/Gmail processes email correspondence; Stripe processes payment information if a qualified buyer is invited to payment. CaptureBrief does not need a full card number to deliver the pilot.

Retention and deletion

The current workflow is designed around public-source material. Inquiry emails and ordinary business records may be retained as reasonably needed to respond, deliver the service, document the engagement, and satisfy legal/accounting/security obligations. You may request deletion or correction of personal information you directly provided by emailing jayp19386@gmail.com; requests may be limited where retention is reasonably required for those obligations.

Security approach

The public site uses HTTPS when hosted on Netlify and is configured with restrictive browser security headers. CaptureBrief relies on access controls provided by its hosting, email, and payment providers. No online system can promise absolute security, and the current founding pilot is intentionally not designed for sensitive procurement information.

Unknown facts

When public evidence does not establish a fact—such as a company-level clearance—the intended behavior is to mark the fact for verification, not infer a positive answer from a weaker signal.

Before a paid pilot

Scope, source coverage, review process, delivery timing, cancellation/refund treatment, deliverable expectations, and the responsible contracting operator are confirmed in writing before billing. If the requested work would require sensitive information, the current founding pilot is not the right workflow.

Construction change intelligence

Turn a scope change into something the commercial team can actually review.

ScopeSignal is an evidence-first construction change workflow: identify a design or field change, connect it to the relevant project record, preserve notice and measurement evidence, and track whether it becomes approved, billable, and paid.

The product

The expensive gap is usually downstream of detection.

Finding that something changed is useful only if the project team can preserve and trace the evidence needed to make a commercial decision.

Revision evidence

Keep old/new source identity and the exact changed region or item.

Commercial authority

Connect the change to the relevant instruction, BOQ/work order, notice, and accepted measurement.

Outcome tracking

Separate potential impact from approved change, certified amount, invoice, and actual payment.

Best fit

Best for contractors and project teams that already lose time or margin between field change and commercial recovery.

  • Scope changes are frequent enough to justify a repeatable process.
  • Notice, measurement, and commercial ownership are currently fragmented.
  • You want evidence continuity rather than another markup viewer.

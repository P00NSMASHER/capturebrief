FAQ

The skeptical questions.

Why not just ask ChatGPT to read the solicitation?

Basic extraction is easy. CaptureBrief uses a defined QA protocol to test the assumptions that survived your primary screen: current source state, supersession, posture-changing gates, and action-changing unknowns. The product earns its fee only when that outside QA adds something consequential beyond the screen you already have.

Why would we use this if we already have capture staff?

That is the intended use case. Your team remains primary. CaptureBrief does not redo your whole capture process; it tests up to five non-sensitive assumptions behind the current gate posture and returns only what the controlling public record supports, contradicts, leaves unproven, supersedes, or cannot safely establish.

Does CaptureBrief make the bid/no-bid decision?

No. It identifies evidence, potential blockers, decision-changing unknowns, and verification actions. Your team keeps the decision.

What if the important company facts are private?

They stay explicitly unverified. Missing public evidence never becomes evidence of failure. The QA tells your team exactly which private fact matters and why it matters.

What if an official attachment cannot be inspected?

The deliverable downgrades or stops. If the controlling package is available only through eBuy, SEWP QRT, another contract-holder portal, or other restricted access, the current public-source product does not ask you to paste or upload it. CaptureBrief does not substitute a mirror, AI summary, or secondary source for unavailable controlling material.

Can CaptureBrief review eBuy, SEWP QRT, or other contract-holder-only task-order packages?

Not in the current public-source founding product. If decision-critical controlling material is restricted to a private or contract-holder portal, CaptureBrief safe-stops rather than asking you to paste restricted content into this workflow.

Why is there still a 14-day watch?

It is included in the founding pilot to test change-impact value, not because 14 days is the long-term product identity. The product is Pursuit QA; the watch exists to see whether the QA baseline stays valid as public sources change.

Is independent GovCon expert calibration complete?

No. CaptureBrief is an outside service relative to your team, but its internal critical-item verification is not independent expert validation. Independent GovCon calibration will be claimed only after a genuinely separate qualified reviewer has reviewed the method and material feedback is recorded.

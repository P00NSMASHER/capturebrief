FAQ

Quick answers.

What exactly do I receive for $149?

One pursuit review: up to five assumptions checked, decision-changing findings and next actions, an appendix tracing the relevant public sources and versions, and a 14-day update watch.

Does the newest rule always control?

No. We record the version relevant to the pursuit and the evidence for that choice. A newer publication does not silently replace it.

What happens when evidence is missing?

The item stays unproven or source-limited, with a specific evidence request. We do not fill gaps with guesses.

Does CaptureBrief tell us whether to bid?

No. It shows what the public record supports, contradicts, leaves unproven, or supersedes. Your team makes the decision. This is not legal advice.

Can you review restricted portal content?

No. The current product is public-source only and stops the affected review when controlling material is not publicly inspectable.

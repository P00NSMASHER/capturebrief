Common questions

Know the boundaries before you start.

Still unsure whether your pursuit fits? Send the public link. There is no charge for the scope check.

What counts as an assumption?

A fact your current plan depends on—for example, “the deadline has not changed,” “we can bid as a prime,” “the response limit is 20 pages,” or “this rule edition applies.”

How fast is the review?

The exact delivery date is confirmed in writing before payment because source volume and deadline pressure vary by pursuit. You can decline if the timing does not work.

What sources do you use?

Public notices, amendments, solicitation files, agency materials, and relevant public rule sources. Official records remain authoritative. If a decision-critical source is private or restricted, the affected finding stays source-limited or the request is declined.

Can I send our proposal, pricing, or portal documents?

No. Do not send CUI, classified or export-controlled material, credentials, proprietary proposal content, nonpublic pricing or strategy, or files from eBuy, SEWP QRT, other contract-holder portals, or private acquisition systems.

Will CaptureBrief tell us to bid or not bid?

No. It tests the assumptions behind your current posture and gives your team an evidence-backed basis for its own GO, HOLD, or PASS decision.

What happens during the 14-day watch?

Agreed public sources are checked on a scheduled basis. A relevant change reopens only the assumptions that depended on it. The watch is not continuous and does not replace your duty to monitor official sources.

What if the opportunity is out of scope?

We decline it before payment. Public-source access, the question set, delivery date, cancellation/refund treatment, and other commercial terms are confirmed in writing first.

Is this legal or procurement advice?

No. CaptureBrief is an independent evidence review, not legal advice, a binding eligibility determination, a compliance certification, or an award guarantee.

Good to know

Plain answers before you start.

Send the public link if you are unsure. The scope check is free.

What counts as an assumption?

A fact your plan depends on, such as the deadline, page limit, eligibility, or which rule edition applies.

How fast is the review?

The delivery date is confirmed after complete intake and before work begins because source volume and deadlines vary.

What sources do you use?

Public notices, amendments, solicitation files, agency pages, and relevant public rule sources. Official records remain authoritative.

Can I send private files?

No. Do not send CUI, credentials, proposal content, pricing, strategy, or files from private acquisition portals.

Will you tell us to bid?

No. CaptureBrief tests the assumptions behind your posture. Your team owns the GO, HOLD, or PASS decision.

Is this legal or procurement advice?

No. It is an independent evidence review, not legal advice, a compliance certification, or an award guarantee.

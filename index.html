Common questions

Here is what to expect.

What does $149 cover?

One public federal IT, cybersecurity or cloud opportunity; up to five assumptions or questions; a short report with document links; and a 14-day watch for relevant public updates. There is no automatic renewal.

What is an “assumption”?

A detail you are counting on. For example: “We qualify to bid” or “The deadline has not changed.” We check what public records support and say when they cannot answer the question.

Will you write our bid or guarantee a win?

No. This is a second look at the facts, not proposal writing, bid submission or legal advice. Your team makes the final decision.

How do we get started?

Email the public opportunity link. We confirm the work and delivery date before payment. Do not send private documents or sensitive company information.

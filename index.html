The product

A QA control surface, not another RFP summary.

CaptureBrief is designed for teams that already have a live opportunity and a preliminary GO / HOLD / NO-GO / UNSURE posture. The job is to attack the factual assumptions the gate call depends on.

01

Controlling packet

Separate current, superseded, corrected, restricted, and supporting public sources instead of treating every official-looking page as equally controlling.

02

Assumption register

Classify what the evidence supports, contradicts, leaves unproven, supersedes, or cannot establish from public sources.

03

Decision impact

Keep gate-changing issues and verify-now unknowns distinct from useful monitor-only context.

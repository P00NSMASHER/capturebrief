FAQ

Quick answers.

What exactly do I receive for $149?

One review of one public federal pursuit: up to five assumptions reviewed, the decision-changing findings, clear next actions, public source links, and a 14-day update watch.

Does CaptureBrief tell us whether to bid?

No. It shows what the public record supports, contradicts, leaves unproven, or supersedes. Your team makes the decision.

Can you review restricted portal content?

No. The current product is public-source only and safe-stops when controlling material is not publicly inspectable.

Why use this if we already have capture staff?

It is a second set of eyes on the assumptions behind the current gate decision, not a replacement for your team.

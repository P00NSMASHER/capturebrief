Assumption QA: what the outside second pass found

Illustrative posture: GO. The fictional capture team believes its August 12 baseline is still controlling. CaptureBrief tests the assumptions behind that screen against the latest public action and amendment chain.

Most important catch: a prior Small Business Pool submitter could not simply reference its old package. Amendment 0011 required a complete resubmission and warned that references to prior submissions may make the proposal nonresponsive.

Assumptions tested

Important: these assumptions are illustrative. They were not supplied by any actual offeror.

“The deadline is still August 12.”SUPERSEDEDUseful confirmation: the latest Small Business Pool deadline moved to August 28, 2026 at 5:00 PM ET.
“We already submitted, so we only need revised pages.”CONTRADICTEDAction-changing: all Small Business Pool offerors had to submit a complete package; references to prior submissions were not accepted.
“The August 28 extension applies to the Unrestricted Pool too.”CONTRADICTEDAction-changing: the Unrestricted Pool was closed to new or revised proposals.
“F5 and Tanium are still Tier 1A OEMs for Small Business.”SUPERSEDEDAction-changing: Amendment 0011 moved both to Tier 2A and revised the related pricing/authorization attachments.
“If a file came from SAM.gov, our older downloaded set is safe to rely on.”CONTRADICTEDAction-changing: the action history includes a locked-publication error, corrected conformed copies, corrected worksheets, and reuploaded amendments.

What the quick screen could miss

Stale official actionOfficial does not always mean current.

An older SAM action remained reachable and authoritative-looking while explicitly warning that newer actions existed.

Resubmission completeness“Just send the changes” could fail.

The latest amendment required the whole Small Business proposal package again.

Pool-specific rightsOne deadline did not apply to both pools.

The Small Business Pool reopened while the Unrestricted Pool stayed closed.

Attachment lineageSame opportunity, different controlling file set.

Corrected and reuploaded files changed what could safely be relied on.

QA handoff

Before acting on this pursuit, prove that the current official action—not merely an official historical action—controls. For Small Business Pool offerors, use the August 28 deadline, rebuild the complete proposal package, use the Amendment 0011 OEM tier structure, and validate every dependency against the corrected attachment set. Unrestricted Pool offerors cannot use the Small Business extension to revise or re-enter.

Source-integrity rule demonstrated

New P0 rule: CaptureBrief must track the opportunity notice and the specific SAM action separately. If an official action says newer actions exist, that action becomes historical/superseded for deadline, eligibility, response-path, and attachment reasoning until the latest action is established.

What the second pass found

Public capability overlap is plausible. The QA does not say “pursue” or “pass.” It says five things need resolution before material proposal effort, and one solicitation nuance prevents an overly simplistic screen.

Most important nuance: missing public evidence of clearance eligibility is not proof of disqualification, and the CSO separates Phase-1 submission permission from award eligibility. A quick screen that collapses those questions could wrongly eliminate a feedback/positioning path.

What a quick screen could get wrong

Wrong“No public FCL proof, so pass.”

Missing public evidence is an unknown company fact, not a negative fact.

Wrong“Award-ineligible means submission-prohibited.”

The CSO indicates a Phase-1 feedback path can exist separately from award eligibility.

Wrong“The parent notice date is the deadline.”

A standing CSO and targeted child calls can have different controlling timing and instructions.

Wrong“A mirrored copy is good enough.”

Supporting copies help inspect text; the current official source must control before reliance.

QA handoff

Before authorizing material proposal effort, verify FCL, TS/SCI staffing capacity, FOCI/OCI posture, and the exact AOI/commercial solution against the current official source baseline. Preserve the Phase-1 feedback/positioning option separately from award eligibility. The customer decides pursue / feedback / hold / pass.

Source integrity

Current public evidence used

  • Official SAM.gov opportunity identity/status
  • Public solicitation text used for requirement inspection
  • Public contractor capability/federal-market sources

Release gate before paid reliance

  • Directly inspect the current official attachment/version
  • Record source/amendment baseline
  • Identify the exact AOI/child call
  • Safe-stop if controlling material is unavailable or materially contradictory

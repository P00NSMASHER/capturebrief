## Acceptance steps for first real supervised free check

1. Independently confirm an actual inbound request and one exact public SAM link, canonical business identity, one assumption and feasible source-specific scope. Privately reserve one of ten slots in the durable reviewed registry; never place customer records in public Git or Actions artifacts.
2. Before accepting work, independently review official current action, full family history and amendments, deleted and current attachments, applicable rules and whether the assumption can be answered. If coverage fails, **hold or decline** rather than claiming no amendments.
3. Provision approved private encrypted operator storage. Copy only an encrypted backup off-device, independently hold its recovery key, and prove genuine recovery on another approved machine.
4. Inspect full Decision Evidence and exact report bytes. The owner must explicitly approve the content and each message or payment invitation before any customer handoff. Source-limited internal review material is **not** a customer report.
5. Privately track source corrections, time and actual feedback. No commercial-proof claim until an actual customer interaction has been separately evidenced.

**Safety boundary:** These tests performed no calls, government inquiry, email, payments, customer enrollment or delivery. Do not bypass core evidence/history/release gates merely to obtain a green readiness label. Cloudflare R2 remains optional, not mandatory.

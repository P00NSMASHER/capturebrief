Privacy

Privacy information

Last updated September 18, 2026

CaptureBrief is currently a founder-operated, supervised founding-stage service focused on public-source federal opportunity qualification. This page describes the public website and current contact workflow; it does not promise future product features.

Operator and contact

CaptureBrief is the working brand used for this founder-operated service. The operator is Jamison Perkins. Applicable business and commercial details are identified in the written scope confirmation before payment. Privacy requests may be sent to jayp19386@gmail.com.

What the website collects

The site includes a narrow Netlify-hosted Pursuit QA intake form. It can collect work email, company, role, public opportunity URL or notice ID, current GO/HOLD/NO-GO/UNSURE posture, an optional public deadline, up to five non-sensitive assumptions behind that posture, and an optional short question. The form does not accept file uploads. Netlify processes the submission so CaptureBrief can scope the request. If URL campaign parameters such as utm_source or utm_campaign are present, the form records them only when you choose to submit, so CaptureBrief can distinguish acquisition sources without an advertising tracker or cross-site cookie. If you separately email CaptureBrief, Google/Gmail processes that correspondence.

Hosting and technical data

Netlify may process ordinary technical request information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, browser/device information, request time, and requested path, according to Netlify's own practices. CaptureBrief does not ask you to include sensitive procurement information merely to browse the site.

Payment provider

If a qualified pilot proceeds, payment may be handled by Stripe through an invitation-only checkout. Stripe processes payment credentials under its own privacy and security practices; CaptureBrief does not need your full card number to provide the service.

What not to send

Do not send CUI, classified information, export-controlled information, credentials, proprietary client material, sensitive proposal content, nonpublic pricing/strategy, protected source-selection information, or material copied from eBuy, SEWP QRT, other contract-holder portals, private acquisition systems, or restricted sources through the intake form or email.

Use, access, and sharing

Information you directly provide is used to respond to your request, assess public-source pilot scope, test the assumptions you asked CaptureBrief to challenge, deliver an agreed engagement, and maintain ordinary business records. Access is limited to the founder/operator and service providers needed for hosting/forms, email, and payment processing. CaptureBrief does not sell personal information to advertisers.

Retention and your requests

Intake submissions, inquiry emails, and ordinary business records may be retained as reasonably needed for communication, service delivery, recordkeeping, security, and legal/accounting obligations. You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you directly provided by emailing the privacy contact above. A request may be limited where retention is reasonably required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or security purposes.

Third-party links

The site links to public external sources for demonstration and verification. Those sites have their own privacy practices.
